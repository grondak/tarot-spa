Supersedes earlier framing of literal card-name referencing.
- (decision) UJ-2 captured (a miss): Maya, a casual non-systems-thinker, weighs buying a car vs continuing to Uber. She types rich, concrete, sensory/nostalgic detail. Draw yields Major Arcana 'The Compression'; the LLM output stays abstract ('What's the minimum viable complexity?') without weaving in her specific concrete details the way Erica's session did. She disengages (makes a face, checks Instagram, wanders off), decides without using the guide, and picks the more complex/luxurious option — arguably the opposite of what the lens implied. Weeks later the phrase resurfaces in her memory unprompted.
- (decision) Quality risk surfaced by UJ-2: essay-form output must weave in the user's own stated concrete details (not just restate the card's abstract idea) to land — an ungrounded/abstract response is a real failure mode, contrasted directly against UJ-1's grounded essay
